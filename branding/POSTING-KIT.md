## 3. What to expect
- **Copyright claim:** Rockstar's trailer footage can get a Content ID claim. That usually means Rockstar gets any ad money from that video, not a strike. The trailer's music isn't in the video (only the narration), so there's no music claim.
- **Shorts views:** Shorts views often arrive in waves over 24–72 hours. Don't judge the channel by the first hour.
- **Launch week:** the next 7 days before GTA 6 launches on 19 Nov are the best window. Aim for 1 Short a day, plus launch-day coverage.
