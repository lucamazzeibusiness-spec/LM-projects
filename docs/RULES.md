# Channel rules

## Content and legal
1. **Only permitted sources:** streamers who said yes in writing, official Rockstar videos, and the channel's own graphics. Keep every permission.
2. **Never leaks.** No leaked clips, maps or builds.
3. **Nothing made up.** Every fact or detail must be verifiable or visible on screen. Rumours only from big outlets, labelled "Gerücht".
4. **AI voice only as a narrator** on footage-led Shorts (official Rockstar footage), never imitating a real person and never on streamer clips. No AI-generated fake game footage.
5. **Follow each streamer's own rules.** No clips that are embarrassing or out of context.
6. **No story spoilers** in titles or thumbnails.
7. **Disclaimer in every description:** "Inoffizieller Fan-Kanal – nicht mit Rockstar Games oder Take-Two verbunden."

## Posting
1. 2–3 Shorts a day at most, at least 3 hours apart.
2. Search words in the title: "GTA 6" plus "deutsch" or a German phrase, plus the streamer's name for clips.
3. Pin a question under every video and reply to comments in the first hour.
4. Nothing uploads automatically. A person checks every clip.

## Credit format for streamer clips
- Title: `… 😂 | STREAMERNAME`
- First line of the description: `🎥 Clip von STREAMERNAME – https://www.twitch.tv/streamername`
- On screen: the channel tag plus a credit line with the streamer's name.

## Format
- Vertical 1080×1920, 15–30 seconds (hard maximum 59 s).
- Hook in the first second.
- Music only from YouTube's library, kept quiet.
